PURSUIT OF PERFECTION

Roasting with Soul, Sourcing with Respect

Founded in 2023, Aurora Coffee Roasters was born from a singular obsession: to elevate coffee from a daily routine into an extraordinary sensory ritual.

We travel directly to regenerative farms in Colombia, Ethiopia, Kenya, and Guatemala, paying on average 280% above Fair Trade minimums. Every harvest lot is cupped, profiled, and roasted in small 12-kilogram batches in our custom cast-iron drum roaster.
